Kick Chaos — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Kick Chaos game (package com.lavidnet.kickchaos, "the app", "we") collects on Android and iOS, why, and the choices you have.

In short: we don't ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location. The game uses an anonymous account id to back up your players and to play online. Game statistics are sent only if you say YES. There are no ads, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.

1. Information the app uses

  • Anonymous account id. The first time you open the game, an anonymous account is created with Unity Gaming Services. It is a random id and contains no name or email address.
  • Game data. Your players' nicknames (which you choose), progress, kits, diamonds and settings. These are stored on your device and backed up to your account's cloud save so you don't lose them.
  • Optional sign-in with Google or Apple. If you choose to log in, the app links your Google or Apple account to your game account so you can restore your players on a new device. We ask only for an opaque account identifier — not your name or email address.
  • Game statistics (only with your consent). If you answer YES when asked (you can change this anytime in Settings), the app sends anonymous gameplay events through Unity Analytics — for example sessions, match results, cup results and which store items were bought. These contain no names and no text you type. Unity may also record standard technical details such as device model, operating system and approximate country. If you say NO, no statistics are collected.
  • Purchases. Diamond packs are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. They handle payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app receives a receipt to verify the purchase and a record of which item was bought.
  • Online and friends play. Online matches are connected through Unity Relay. The other players in a match see your player's nickname and the moves of the game. No chat or personal data is exchanged.
  • Problem reports (only if you send one). If you use "report a problem", we receive the text you write, the app version, device model, operating system, app language and your player's nickname, so we can fix the issue.

2. What we don't collect

  • No email address, phone number, real name, contacts, photos or location.
  • No advertising id and no ads of any kind.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

3. How the information is used

  • To run the game, back up and restore your players, and connect online matches.
  • To verify purchases and deliver diamonds.
  • With your consent, to understand how the game is played and improve it.
  • To answer and fix problem reports.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising. Data is processed only by the service providers that make the game work:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: authentication, cloud save, relay, analytics) — Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google (Google Play purchases and optional Sign in with Google) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple (App Store purchases and optional Sign in with Apple, on iOS) — Apple Privacy Policy

We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Security

Data sent between the app and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to stored data is limited to what is needed to run and support the game.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your game data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all its cloud data at any time inside the app: Settings → DELETE ACCOUNT. Deletion removes your cloud save and account; if you had signed in with Apple, the app also revokes that link. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing us (see below). You can stop game statistics at any time in Settings; analytics data already collected is kept by Unity according to its retention rules.

7. Children

Kick Chaos is made for players of all ages, including children under 13. We take care to follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR's rules for children, and Google Play's Families Policy:

  • The app does not ask children for their name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location.
  • There are no ads, no advertising id, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Game statistics are off unless the player says YES, and they contain no personal information. A parent can turn them off at any time in Settings.
  • Player nicknames are chosen in the game. We ask children not to use their real name as a nickname.
  • There is no chat and no way for other players to send messages.
  • Buying diamonds goes through Google Play or the App Store, where parents can require approval for every purchase (for example with Google Family Link or Apple's Ask to Buy).

Parents: you can delete your child's account and all its data at any time in the app (Settings → DELETE ACCOUNT), or email us and we will delete it for you. If you believe your child has sent us personal information (for example by typing it into a problem report), contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent. Most of this you can do directly in the app; for anything else, email us.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
